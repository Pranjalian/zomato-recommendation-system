# PROBLEM STATEMENT: Zomato Recommendation System Prototype

## CONTEXT & VISION
The modern food delivery ecosystem is characterized by an overwhelming abundance of choices, leading to severe decision fatigue for consumers. While expansive catalogs offer variety, they often hinder quick and satisfying decision-making. Tackling this challenge is a dream project, serving as an ideal prototype to practice and apply GenAI and AscendAI capabilities within this generative AI program. By building a simple but highly effective Zomato use case, we can demonstrate how intelligent systems transform user experience.

## THE PROBLEM
Users frequently open the Zomato app with a vague intent (e.g., "I'm hungry for something spicy") but struggle to pinpoint a specific restaurant or dish among thousands of options. This "choice overload" results in:
1. Increased time-to-order, leading to user frustration.
2. Higher app drop-off and cart abandonment rates.
3. Habitual ordering from the same 2-3 restaurants, missing out on the platform's broader variety.

Current static listing methods fail to account for real-time contextual factors like weather, exact time of day, precise user location, and immediate dietary cravings.

## OBJECTIVE
To develop a smart, generative AI-powered recommendation system prototype for Zomato that actively curates and suggests restaurants. The system will process user preferences, implicit feedback, and contextual data to provide highly personalized, explainable recommendations, effectively reducing the cognitive load on the user.

## EXPECTED IMPACT
By implementing this recommendation use case, the prototype will:
- Streamline the discovery process, helping users find preferable restaurants instantly.
- Demonstrate the practical application of GenAI and AscendAI in solving real-world marketplace challenges.
- Provide a scalable foundation for understanding complex user-item interactions in a highly dynamic, time-sensitive environment.
